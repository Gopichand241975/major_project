#READ ME
**Gait Recognition** is a biometric technology that identifies or verifies a person based on their unique walking pattern. It uses computer vision and artificial intelligence to analyze the movement of a person's body from video captured by a camera. Unlike face recognition, gait recognition can identify individuals even when their face is not visible or they are wearing a mask. It is widely used in security surveillance, access control, healthcare monitoring, and smart city applications. The system works by capturing a walking video, extracting gait features, and comparing them with stored gait patterns to recognize the person accurately.
